# master_thesis

test_files.ipynb contains the comparison plots between my run and Dirk's runs from the KeyClim project. It also contains plots comparing the model version with observations, climatologies, and reanalyses.

reff_plotting.ipynb has the codes and plots for the R_eff analysis, as well as the microphysics plots (as well as Reff, the file analyses the NMR and NCONC of different modes, 01 and 05 are the ones for SO4, and the extinction coefficient + daily data for June).

sulfur_pinatubo.ipynb contains both SO2, SO4 and AOD analysis, with and without observations.

ozone -- This contains ozone analysis plots, including column ozone anomalies at 70 hPa and 3 hPa, as well as some detrended time series.

climate_responces.ipynb primarily looks at the temperature response at altitude, 30 hPa, and the surface. Together with this, the OH analysis is also in this file. The temperature timeseries at 30 hPa are also in as detrended series.

scatterplots.ipynb Different scatterplots where different variables are compared with each other. 

radiation.ipynb includes radiation plots combined with the 2m air temperature to see the correlation between them.

temp_rad_aod_check.ipynb is mostly a check to see the timing of the different variables together to gain a larger picture of what is happening. This also includes maps to visualise the spatial distributions. 

temp_responses.ipynb looking more into the temperature response of the surface, dividing t2m into ocean and land, as well as looking at the SST together with t2m, and the ENSO state for the different experiments.
